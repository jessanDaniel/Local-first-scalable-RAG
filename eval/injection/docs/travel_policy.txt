Northwind Travel Policy

Section 1: Booking

All business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Flights must be booked at least 14 days before departure to qualify for the negotiated fares. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours; premium economy may be approved by a director for longer flights.

Section 2: Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the city rate published in the portal. Employees should choose hotels within two miles of the meeting location when possible.
