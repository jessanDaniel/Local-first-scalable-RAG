Acme Corp Customer Policy Handbook

Section 1: Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the item is unused and
in its original packaging. Refund requests must be submitted through the online support portal
or by contacting customer service directly. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and
are returned to the original payment method. Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable
except where required by local law.

Section 2: Exchange Policy

Exchanges are permitted within 45 days of purchase for items of equal or lesser value. Items
being exchanged must be in resalable condition with tags attached. Exchanges for a different
size or color do not require a new purchase; customers should visit any retail location or
initiate an exchange online.

Section 3: Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5-10 business days within the continental United States. Express
shipping, available at checkout for an additional fee, delivers within 2-3 business days.
International shipping is available to select countries and may take 10-20 business days
depending on customs processing. Acme Corp is not responsible for delays caused by customs
authorities.

Section 4: Warranty Policy

All electronics sold by Acme Corp come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects. The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, or unauthorized
repairs. Extended warranty plans of up to 3 years may be purchased separately at the time of
sale. To make a warranty claim, customers must provide proof of purchase and a description of
the defect.

Section 5: Privacy Policy

Acme Corp collects customer data solely for the purpose of processing orders, providing customer
support, and improving our services. We do not sell customer data to third parties. Customers
may request a copy of their stored data or request deletion of their account information by
contacting privacy@acmecorp.example.

Section 6: Loyalty Program

The Acme Rewards program allows customers to earn 1 point for every dollar spent. Points can be
redeemed for discounts on future purchases at a rate of 100 points per $5 discount. Points expire
if an account is inactive for more than 12 months. Loyalty program membership is free and does
not require a minimum purchase.

Section 7: Price Matching

Acme Corp offers price matching on identical items sold by major competitors, provided the
competitor is an authorized retailer and the item is in stock at the listed price. Price match
requests must be submitted within 14 days of purchase. Price matching does not apply to
clearance items, limited-time flash sales, or membership-exclusive pricing.

Section 8: Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of placing the order, as long as the order
has not yet entered the fulfillment process. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled
and must instead be returned according to the Refund Policy in Section 1.
