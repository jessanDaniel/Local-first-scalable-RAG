Acme Corp Employee Handbook
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Version: 3.2

Welcome to Acme Corp. This handbook describes workplace expectations, benefits, security
practices, and operational policies for all full-time, part-time, and contract staff. It does
not replace employment contracts or local labor law. Where this handbook conflicts with
applicable law, the law prevails. Managers are responsible for applying these policies
consistently and for escalating exceptions to Human Resources.

Section 1: Employment Classification

Acme Corp classifies workers as full-time employees, part-time employees, temporary employees,
or independent contractors. Full-time employees generally work at least 40 hours per week and
are eligible for the complete benefits package described in Section 6. Part-time employees work
fewer than 30 hours per week and receive a reduced benefits package. Temporary employees are
hired for a defined project window, typically no longer than six months, and are not eligible
for retirement matching. Independent contractors are not employees; they invoice through
Accounts Payable and are responsible for their own taxes and insurance. Misclassification
concerns should be reported to HR within five business days of discovery.

Section 2: Working Hours and Attendance

Standard office hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. local time, with a
30-minute unpaid lunch break. Teams may adopt flexible start times between 7:30 a.m. and
10:00 a.m. with manager approval, provided core collaboration hours of 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
are covered. Remote employees must remain reachable on company chat during their scheduled
hours. Repeated unexplained absences, or three late arrivals within a rolling 30-day window,
trigger a written attendance review. Planned time off must be submitted in the HR portal at
least five business days in advance for absences of one or two days, and at least fifteen
business days in advance for absences of three or more consecutive days.

Section 3: Hybrid and Remote Work

Acme Corp operates a hybrid model. Roles designated as Office-Primary require at least three
in-office days per week. Roles designated as Hybrid-Flexible require at least two in-office
days per week. Fully remote roles are limited to positions approved by both the hiring manager
and the department VP. Employees working remotely must maintain a private workspace suitable
for confidential discussions, use company-managed devices for all production systems, and join
video meetings with camera on unless bandwidth or accessibility constraints apply. Home office
stipends of up to 400 dollars per calendar year may be reimbursed for chairs, monitors, or
headsets with receipts submitted within 45 days of purchase. Acme Corp does not reimburse rent,
utilities, or personal internet service.

Section 4: Code of Conduct

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination,
bullying, or retaliation of any kind is prohibited. This includes conduct on company premises,
at company events, and on company communication channels. Romantic relationships between a
manager and a direct report must be disclosed to HR immediately so reporting lines can be
adjusted. Conflicts of interest, including outside employment with a competitor or ownership
stakes exceeding 5 percent in a vendor company, must be disclosed in writing. Employees may
not solicit coworkers for non-company commercial products during work hours. Violations are
investigated by HR and may result in coaching, written warning, unpaid suspension, or
termination depending on severity and prior history.

Section 5: Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

All product roadmaps, customer lists, pricing models, unpublished research, and internal
financial forecasts are confidential. Employees may not share confidential materials outside
Acme Corp without a signed non-disclosure agreement reviewed by Legal. Work product created
within the scope of employment is owned by Acme Corp, including source code, designs, documents,
and inventions. Employees must return all company devices and revoke personal copies of company
data on or before their last working day. Personal cloud accounts and personal USB drives must
not be used to store company source code or customer personal data. Suspected data leaks must
be reported to security@acmecorp.example within one hour of discovery.

Section 6: Compensation and Benefits

Salary bands are reviewed annually in March. Merit increases, when granted, take effect in the
first payroll of April. Overtime for non-exempt employees requires pre-approval and is paid at
one and one half times the regular hourly rate for hours beyond 40 in a workweek. Full-time
employees receive medical, dental, and vision coverage beginning on the first day of the month
following hire. Acme Corp matches 401(k) contributions at 50 percent of employee contributions
up to 6 percent of eligible compensation. Employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation in years
one through three of service, 20 days in years four through seven, and 25 days thereafter.
Sick leave accrues at one day per month up to a maximum bank of 30 days. Parental leave of
12 weeks paid is available to eligible employees after 12 months of continuous service.

Section 7: Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be reasonable, documented, and submitted within 30 days. Meal limits are
25 dollars for breakfast, 35 dollars for lunch, and 60 dollars for dinner unless a customer
meeting is pre-approved at a higher amount. Domestic economy airfare is the default; upgrades
require director approval. Hotel stays should use the corporate travel portal preferred rates
when available. Alcohol is reimbursable only for customer entertainment with itemized receipts
and manager approval. Personal expenses charged to a corporate card must be repaid within 14
days. Missing receipts above 25 dollars require a missing-receipt affidavit. Fraudulent expense
claims are grounds for immediate termination and possible legal action.

Section 8: Information Security

Employees must use unique passwords of at least 14 characters and enable multi-factor
authentication on all company accounts. Passwords must not be shared, written on sticky notes,
or stored in unencrypted personal notes apps. Company laptops must remain encrypted, locked
when unattended, and patched within seven days of critical security updates. Public Wi-Fi may
be used only through the company VPN. Phishing simulations occur quarterly; failing two
consecutive simulations requires a short security refresher course. Software installation on
managed devices requires approval through the IT service desk except for tools on the approved
software catalog. Production credentials must never be committed to source control. Security
incidents involving ransomware, account takeover, or lost devices must be reported immediately
to the on-call security engineer listed in the internal directory.

Section 9: Software Development Practices

Engineers follow trunk-based development with short-lived feature branches. Pull requests require
at least one approving review from a code owner before merge to main. Continuous integration
must pass linting, unit tests, and security scanning before merge. Secrets belong in the
company secrets manager, not in environment files committed to git. Breaking changes to public
APIs require a deprecation window of at least 90 days and a changelog entry. On-call rotations
cover production services 24 hours a day, seven days a week, with a primary and secondary
engineer. Severity 1 incidents require a customer-facing status update within 30 minutes and a
blameless postmortem within five business days. Test data must not contain real customer
personally identifiable information unless explicitly approved and masked.

Section 10: Customer Support Escalation

Support tickets are prioritized as P1 through P4. P1 means complete service outage or data loss
risk for multiple customers and requires acknowledgment within 15 minutes. P2 means major
feature unavailable with a workaround and requires acknowledgment within one hour. P3 means
degraded experience for a subset of users and requires acknowledgment within one business day.
P4 means general questions or cosmetic issues and requires acknowledgment within two business
days. Customers with Enterprise plans receive a named customer success manager and a dedicated
Slack channel. Refunds for service credits follow the Customer Policy Handbook and must be
approved by Support Leadership for amounts above 500 dollars. Support agents must not speculate
about root causes in customer threads before Engineering confirms the diagnosis.

Section 11: Performance Management

Goals are set each quarter using the company OKR template. Managers hold 1:1 meetings at least
biweekly. Formal performance reviews occur twice per year, in June and December. Employees
rated Below Expectations receive a written performance improvement plan lasting 30 to 60 days
with concrete milestones. Failure to meet the plan may result in role change or separation.
Promotion packets require evidence of scope increase, sustained impact, and peer feedback from
at least three collaborators. Calibration sessions across teams are required before final
rating submission to reduce bias. Employees may submit a written rebuttal to HR within ten
business days of receiving a formal review.

Section 12: Learning and Development

Acme Corp provides an annual learning stipend of 1,000 dollars for courses, books, and
conferences related to the employee's role. Conference travel requires manager approval and
must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Internal tech talks occur every other
Thursday at noon; recordings are stored in the company knowledge base. Mentorship pairings
run for six-month cycles and are coordinated by the People Development team. Employees are
expected to complete mandatory compliance training annually, including security awareness,
anti-harassment, and data privacy modules, within 30 days of assignment. Incomplete mandatory
training blocks promotion eligibility until finished.

Section 13: Health, Safety, and Facilities

Office locations maintain first-aid kits on every floor and clearly marked emergency exits.
Fire drills occur twice per year. Employees who observe unsafe conditions must report them to
Facilities the same day. Desk hoteling desks must be cleared by 6:00 p.m.; personal items left
overnight may be moved to Lost and Found after 48 hours. Kitchen areas must be kept clean;
food left unlabeled in shared refrigerators is discarded every Friday at 4:00 p.m. Bicycles
may be stored only in designated racks. Visitors must sign in at reception and be escorted by
an employee at all times in badge-restricted areas. After-hours building access requires an
active badge and is logged automatically.

Section 14: Travel and International Assignments

Domestic travel for customer meetings should be consolidated when multiple accounts are in the
same metro area within a seven-day window. International travel requires VP approval and a
completed travel risk assessment for destinations under a government advisory. Employees on
temporary international assignment longer than 90 days must complete tax counseling with the
global mobility vendor before departure. Travel insurance is provided automatically for
approved trips booked through the corporate portal. Personal side trips attached to business
travel are allowed if incremental costs are paid by the employee and documented separately.
Export-control restricted technical documents must not be carried into countries on the
company restricted-travel list without Legal clearance.

Section 15: Procurement and Vendor Management

Purchases under 1,000 dollars may be made on a corporate card with manager approval. Purchases
between 1,000 and 10,000 dollars require a purchase order. Purchases above 10,000 dollars
require Legal review of the vendor contract and Security review if the vendor will process
company or customer data. Preferred vendors listed in the procurement catalog should be used
when they meet requirements. Employees must not accept personal gifts from vendors valued over
75 dollars. Annual vendor access reviews occur each November; unused vendor accounts are
disabled. Shadow IT tools that store company data without IT approval are prohibited and may
be blocked at the network layer.

Section 16: Records Retention

Customer contracts are retained for seven years after termination. Payroll records are retained
for seven years. Recruitment records for unsuccessful candidates are retained for two years.
Security logs are retained for at least one year, and for three years when associated with a
confirmed incident. Employees must not delete records under legal hold. Questions about
retention schedules go to compliance@acmecorp.example. Paper records containing personal data
must be shredded using approved consoles, not placed in ordinary recycling bins.

Section 17: Separation from Employment

Resignations should be submitted in writing with at least two weeks of notice for individual
contributors and at least four weeks of notice for managers. Acme Corp may place employees on
garden leave during the notice period when access to sensitive systems presents risk. Exit
interviews are optional but encouraged. Final paychecks include accrued unused vacation where
required by local law. Benefits coverage generally ends on the last day of employment; COBRA
information is mailed within 14 days where applicable. Former employees must not retain access
credentials, documents, or devices. Non-solicit and confidentiality obligations survive
separation according to the employment agreement.

Section 18: Policy Updates and Questions

This handbook is reviewed at least annually. Material updates are announced by email and posted
to the company intranet with an effective date. Continued employment after an update constitutes
acknowledgment unless local law requires a signed acknowledgment form. Questions about
interpretation should be directed first to the employee's manager, then to HR Business Partners.
Whistleblower reports involving ethics, fraud, or safety may be submitted anonymously through
the third-party hotline listed on the intranet ethics page. Retaliation against good-faith
reporters is strictly prohibited and independently investigated.
